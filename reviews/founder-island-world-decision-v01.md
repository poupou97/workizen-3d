# Founder Island World Decision v01

Date: 2026-06-08  
Project: Workizen 3D  
Decision owner: Founder

## Founder Decision

Workizen HQ Campus is approved as a starter island in the middle of the ocean.

The canonical direction is no longer an infinite grass plane. Future implementation should make the world read as Workizen HQ Island.

## Why Island World Is Approved

The island direction better supports the approved Workizen mood:

- Bright
- Optimistic
- Friendly
- Cute low-poly
- Animal Crossing + Zepeto + modern startup campus

It gives the campus a clear boundary, a memorable first impression, and a stronger identity than an open flat prototype.

## What Problem It Solves

The island direction solves these issues:

- Infinite grass plane reads as generic Three.js prototype.
- Sparse world edges make the campus feel unfinished.
- Large UI panels currently carry too much explanation.
- Districts need a stronger environmental composition.
- Users should feel they arrived at Workizen HQ, not an abstract demo map.

## What Implementation Should Do Next

Next implementation should:

- Add ocean boundary.
- Add golden beach ring.
- Shape the campus as an island terrain.
- Replace prototype ground feeling with intentional island composition.
- Use stone/tile paths to connect districts.
- Increase trees, flowers, bushes, benches, lamps, and waterfront details.
- Prepare bottom navigation.
- Prepare compact left info cards for selected context.

## What Must Not Be Done Yet

Do not implement yet:

- NPC walking
- Animation
- Pathfinding
- Multiplayer
- Backend
- Economy
- Marketplace logic
- New gameplay systems

The next step should remain a visual/world composition pass.

# Workizen Next Sprints

Status: Planning  
Project: Workizen 3D  
Date: 2026-06-08

## Phase 2E - Island World Composition Sprint

Goal: Convert current HQ Campus from flat prototype campus into Workizen HQ Island.

## Scope

Phase 2E should focus on world composition only:

- Ocean boundary
- Beach ring
- Stone paths
- Island terrain
- Dense trees and flowers
- Pier, pond, or bridge where appropriate
- Bottom navigation preparation
- Compact info card preparation

## Do Not Include

Do not implement:

- NPC walking
- Animation
- Backend
- Multiplayer
- Economy

## Expected Outcome

Workizen HQ should read as a cute, bright, low-poly starter island in the ocean.

The scene should make the Founder and first-time users immediately understand:

- This is Workizen HQ Island.
- Citizen Plaza is the starting point.
- Districts are connected by visible paths.
- The world carries identity before the UI explains it.
